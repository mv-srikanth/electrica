Film 01 · Transformers

Prim & Sec: a transformer talk

Single-phase transformer · about 5 minutes · with voices

About this film

The primary and secondary windings of a transformer, Prim and Sec, talk each other through how the machine works. The jokes are theirs; the physics is exact.

What it covers

  • Why the two windings never touch, and what links them
  • The EMF equation, E = 4.44 f N Φm
  • Turns ratio, voltage ratio and current ratio
  • Ampere-turn balance when the load comes on
  • A worked example: 1 kVA, 230 / 115 V
